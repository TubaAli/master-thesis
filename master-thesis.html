Ecocentric AI-Agent Societies

Advisor: Ira Winder · Committee: Catharina Maracke, Joi Ito

1. What do I want to be an expert in?

Designing and evaluating AI-agent societies whose values account for non-human life, applied to regenerative economies.

  1. AI for regenerative economics: economies where AI agents treat nature as a stakeholder.
  2. AI-agent societies: simulating how multi-agent systems evolve norms, governance and values.
  3. Ecocentric AI alignment: methods (value constitutions first) that make models weigh ecological interests.

2. What question is worth answering, and for whom?

In a simulated decentralized society where nature is an economic stakeholder, how do LLM agents with ecocentric vs. anthropocentric constitutions compare, with each other and with humans, in human welfare, value drift and governance outcomes?

Sub-questions

RQTypeQuestion
RQ1MeasureHow do current LLMs trade off human and ecological interests compared with humans? (scenario game, 100–300 participants)
RQ2SimulateOver many generations, do ecocentric values persist or drift, and which governance rules does each society adopt?
RQ3Trade-offDoes an ecocentric constitution cost human welfare, or can humans and nature do better together?

4. Open gaps

  1. Add ecosystem health as a metric. An ecocentric thesis needs it, or RQ3 can't be answered.
  2. Human study: needs a budget and ethics approval. Start both early.
  3. Define "ecocentric" from the literature (e.g., deep ecology, Rights of Nature).
  4. Novelty: position against prior LLM-agent commons simulations (e.g., GovSim).
