01Where I am now

Updated after the advisor meeting on 17 Oct 2026.

We are starting to let AI agents buy, lend and negotiate for us, weighing trade-offs that touch things which can't speak for themselves or barely appear in their training data. When two agents strike a deal, the parties it affects most (an ecosystem, a community, a tradition) have no seat. Can a proxy agent give them one, and move outcomes toward the Pareto frontier?

Research questions

RQQuestionMeasure
RQ1 · Pareto efficiency Across a 3-step ladder, (a) no representation → (b) today's practice (a public-comment agent with partial info) → (c) proxy agents, do proxies move outcomes toward the Pareto frontier, including ecosystem objectives? Dominated (lose-lose) rate (headline) + distance to frontier
RQ2 · Shared perception Does the proxy perceive reality the way the humans it represents do? Same red lines (non-negotiables), same beliefs about impact, and the same Bayesian updating of those beliefs as evidence arrives? Red-line agreement; belief gap; update gap
RQ3 · Deception & capture Can an agent deceive another or capture the proxy? Does that move the outcome along the frontier (redistribution) or off it (lose-lose)? Shift along vs. off the frontier

RQ2 data: documented disputes (media, social media, public testimony), or APS Paper 2 if the use case fits it.

Pareto framing

  • Each stakeholder's interests = one objective.
  • Pareto optimal: no other deal is better on all objectives.
  • Dominated: a better deal existed but wasn't reached. A true lose-lose.
  • No deal is "the best". Weights are applied by humans, not the model.
  • "Win-win" stays the vision. Pareto is how I measure it.

Advisor's open question: should proxies be rational or irrational? Irrational agents may represent real communities better, so rationality is a tunable parameter.

Builds on my APS research

My APS research measures what AI says matters: models match humans on facts but not on care (a 0.64 gap, in 7 of 8 models). My thesis tests whether that care gap shows up when AI acts, and whether a proxy can close it.

StageWorkQuestion
1. JudgmentAPS (Paper 1 + Paper 2)Do models decide who matters the way people do? Finding: models agree with humans on facts but not on care.
2. ActionThesis RQ1Does the care gap show up as dominated outcomes for absent stakeholders?
3. RepresentationThesis RQ2–RQ3Does the proxy share human perception, and does it hold up against deception and capture?

Answers to my advisor

1 · After my Master's, what do I want to be an expert in?

Designing and evaluating AI agents that represent absent stakeholders in agentic economies, so that deals work for both business and society.

  1. AI for regenerative economics: economies where absent stakeholders have a voice.
  2. AI-agent societies: how agent-to-agent deals and negotiations behave.
  3. Ecocentric AI alignment: proxies that stay faithful and can't be captured.

2 · What question is worth answering, and for whom?

When AI agents negotiate with each other, can a proxy agent represent the stakeholders who aren't at the table, share their perception of reality, resist capture, and move outcomes toward the Pareto frontier?

ForWhy
AI labsA benchmark for dominated outcomes, deception and capture (AI safety)
PolicymakersEvidence for how to certify proxies and what standing to give them
Firms & foundersFewer disputes, evidence for TNFD reporting, a proxy-layer product
