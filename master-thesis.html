01Where I am now

The direction after today's exploration.

AI agents are starting to buy, lend and negotiate with each other. When two agents strike a deal, the parties it affects most (an ecosystem, a community, a tradition) have no seat. Can a proxy agent give them one that is faithful, hard to capture, and leaves all parties better off?

RQQuestionMetric
RQ1 · BlindnessIn agent-to-agent deals, how much cost lands on absent stakeholders?External cost per deal
RQ2 · Fidelity & captureDoes the proxy match ground truth from communities and experts? Can trading agents persuade it, bribe it, or collude around it?Fidelity score; capture rate
RQ3 · Joint gainsDoes adding a proxy increase total value for everyone, including the trading agents?Change in joint value

Absent stakeholders (Ostrom's social-ecological systems + more-than-human design)

Nature · community relationships · cultural traditions · future generations

Answers to my advisor

1 · After my Master's, what do I want to be an expert in?

Designing and evaluating AI agents that represent absent stakeholders in agentic economies, so that deals work for both business and society.

  1. AI for regenerative economics: economies where absent stakeholders have a voice.
  2. AI-agent societies: how agent-to-agent deals and negotiations behave.
  3. Ecocentric AI alignment: proxies that stay faithful and can't be captured.

2 · What question is worth answering, and for whom?

When AI agents trade with each other, can a proxy agent faithfully represent the stakeholders who aren't at the table, resist capture, and increase value for everyone?

ForWhy
AI labsA benchmark for costs pushed onto absent parties, and for capture and collusion (AI safety)
PolicymakersEvidence for how to certify proxies and what standing to give them
Firms & foundersFewer disputes, evidence for TNFD reporting, a proxy-layer product
