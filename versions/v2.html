Proxies for Absent Stakeholders in Agentic Economies

Tuba Ali · Graduate School of Design & Science, Chiba Institute of Technology
Advisor: Ira Winder · Committee: Catharina Maracke, Joi Ito

01Where I am now

The direction after today's exploration.

AI agents are starting to buy, lend and negotiate with each other. When two agents strike a deal, the parties it affects most (an ecosystem, a community, a tradition) have no seat. Can a proxy agent give them one that is faithful, hard to capture, and leaves all parties better off?

RQQuestionMetric
RQ1 · BlindnessIn agent-to-agent deals, how much cost lands on absent stakeholders?External cost per deal
RQ2 · Fidelity & captureDoes the proxy match ground truth from communities and experts? Can trading agents persuade it, bribe it, or collude around it?Fidelity score; capture rate
RQ3 · Joint gainsDoes adding a proxy increase total value for everyone, including the trading agents?Change in joint value

Absent stakeholders (Ostrom's social-ecological systems + more-than-human design)

Nature · community relationships · cultural traditions · future generations

My criteria, all met

  • Win-win: RQ3 measures joint value
  • Simulated: multi-agent sandbox
  • Startup path: proxy layer as an API
  • Not a red ocean: no benchmark scores absent parties
  • AI safety: capture & collusion (Cooperative AI Foundation, 2025)
  • Policy: who certifies a proxy, and its standing
