It's already happening

We are starting to let AI agents buy, lend and negotiate for us.

Every deal is a trade-off. Some of those trade-offs touch things that can't speak for themselves.

The problem

When two agents make a deal, who is missing from the table?

🌳

Nature

Ecosystems, water, species
🏘️

Local communities

Residents, neighbourhood ties
⛩️

Cultural traditions

Sacred sites, festivals, crafts
👶

Future generations

People not yet born

I call them absent stakeholders: affected by the deal, but with no seat.

Why AI won't fix this by itself

AI knows the facts, but doesn't care like people do

My APS research
0.64
gap between AI and humans on care, while AI matches humans on facts (7 of 8 models)
Chen et al., Nature Human Behaviour 2026
48%
AI personas got cultural patterns right only about half the time, a coin flip, and did worse in less-connected countries

The people most likely to be left out are the ones AI understands least.

My idea

Give absent stakeholders a seat, through a proxy agent that speaks for them in the deal.

🏢 Company agent⇄🏛️ Town agent⇄🛡️ Proxy agent

The proxy informs real consultation. It never replaces it.

How I judge a deal

Not "the best deal", just no lose-lose

Frontier: no other deal is better for everyone.
Below the line: a better deal existed but was missed.
Weights stay with humans, not with the model.

Research questions

Four questions

RQ1 · Pareto

Do proxies move deals toward the frontier?

Compared with no representation and with today's practice
RQ2 · Fidelity

Does the proxy see things like the people it represents?

Red lines, beliefs, how it updates, and whether people notice drift
RQ3 · Control & capture

Who steers the proxy?

Can deception or capture push the deal off the frontier?
RQ4 · Human flourishing

Do people feel more heard, or replaced?

A small pilot survey

Past research vs. my gap

What's new here?

RQ1 · Fully new

Scoring outsiders

No study scores the parties outside the deal.
RQ2 · New setting

Grounded proxy

AI-vs-human checks exist, but not for a proxy in a deal built from real testimony.
RQ3 · New setting

Capturing an outsider's proxy

Hijacking tests exist, but not for a proxy speaking for outsiders.
RQ4 · New setting

Acting, not writing

Feeling represented is tested for AI statements, not AI that acts for you.

RQ2–4 borrow tested methods. RQ1 is the headline.
